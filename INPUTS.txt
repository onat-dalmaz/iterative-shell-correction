Inputs

The manifests name the exact three phantom conditions and five volunteer cases.
Resolve their relative filenames under --data. The release examples provide
standard_autoshim and S03; the other cases and saved image tiles are not included.
Known source SHA-256 values are checked before loading.

volume.npy and the saved measured-field CP reference are complex64 [z,x,y].
The public slice axes are [z,row,column]; no transpose or flip is applied.
timing.npy is float32 in seconds on the same centered k-space grid, not an
image-space time map. The published geometry is 112×768×768, with voxel spacing
[1.5,0.546875,0.546875] mm. Physical field Hz means the acquired phase is
exp(-i*2*pi*f*t); a prepared field must already have this sign and registration.
The retained workflows use the configured saved CP reference and fail if it is
missing. They never infer a field sign, register a field, or replace the reference.

Image metrics use the original broad breast mask, float32 magnitude matching
to the uncorrected image on every fourth nonempty-mask slice, and each fixed
reference_range. Reference gains are fixed in volunteers.json. saved_gains
record the original method gains; scoring also returns the recomputed gains.
Figure 4B instead uses anterior_roi intersected with measured-field tissue
support and the per-slice 55-pixel body-surface band, sampled every second
in-plane voxel. Estimated fields are averaged 4×3×3 before evaluation. The
signed bin means are averaged equally across five volunteers before taking
their absolute value. This is not MAE. effective_field_hz.npy includes the
final shell-phase contribution; accumulated_field_hz.npy is the CP-driving map
shown in Figure 5.

Phantom labels use the fixed 25 tiles on z=56, the primary geometric/tissue
mask, no fitted gain, and the mean finite tile PSNR. The displayed slice is z=53.
Packed phantom masks are uint8, unpacked along axis 1. All other masks are bool.

Plotting is separate from rerunning correction. data/figure4.json contains the
reported scalar results and can be plotted without MRI data. For Figures 2, 3
and 5, --assets must contain the private saved PNG tiles listed, with hashes, in
settings/figure_images.json. Those display assets retain the published windows,
support and crops. The SVG templates contain no MRI images. Figure locations
and display windows are in settings/figure_locations.json. Plotting does not
choose new patches or alter data. A rerun Figure 4 can use --scores with the
figure4.json produced by the scoring command.

Example ZIPs include their own manifests. These retain the complete cohort recipe
so --case can select the supplied case. The S03 example ROI archive contains only
the numeric mask, with exactly the original voxels; its container checksum differs
from the original archive. The canonical settings/ manifests remain unchanged.
